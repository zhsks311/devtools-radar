OBS or Action! for screen recording? Compare cost and license rights

Start with whether a free recorder meets your needs and whether your recordings will earn money. This is a source-based buying guide, not a hands-on performance review.

Choose a path first

  • Record or stream without paying: consider OBS Studio first. It is free and open source on Windows, macOS and Linux, with scenes and multiple video/audio sources. Official OBS features and downloads
  • Need Action! on Windows: try the official trial with your own game, microphone and system audio before purchasing a license.
  • Revenue-producing video or business work: Home and Commercial rights differ. We do not recommend an unspecified commercial license simply because its price is lower.

Three checks before spending

  1. Make a short recording of your intended screen or game; check microphone and system audio.
  2. Check new-user, device-count and commercial-use restrictions. Ask the seller before redemption if the terms are unclear.
  3. Check when refunds stop and compare the final bill including optional services and taxes.
