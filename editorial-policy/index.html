Editorial and source policy

A comparison is a starting point for a decision, not a substitute for the vendor's terms or a live checkout.

What this site is

DevTeardown publishes developer-tool comparisons and a multilingual catalog of product and software offers. The publisher is identified by this site brand. Brand authorship does not imply a named independent expert or a laboratory test.

Sources and calculations

Comparison articles link to their source material. Published vendor documentation and pricing are the basis for source-based claims. Cost examples use stated assumptions; they are not measured performance benchmarks or guaranteed invoices. A conclusion or recommendation is editorial interpretation, not a vendor endorsement.

A recorded source-review date describes the date stored with an article. It does not mean every linked page was rechecked on the day you visit. Rebuilding or translating a page is not a fresh verification. Follow the original sources for current limits, currency, tax, regional pricing and terms.

Deals and purchase eligibility

A deal-source link explains where an offer was found; a product link leads to the seller. Neither alone proves current stock or a successful purchase. Displayed prices are observations, not live quotes. A manufacturer listing without a verified discount must not be read as a confirmed bargain.

Language and purchase country are separate. A translated page does not establish delivery, activation, payment eligibility or local warranty. Read each offer's regional conditions. Unknown eligibility is not approval to buy. Check the seller's checkout before paying.

Editorial and shopping links

Shopping links are identified separately from comparison prose. Some shopping links may be affiliate links where disclosed; a link or inclusion is not a guarantee of quality. This site does not ask you to rely on a fabricated customer rating, test result or personal-use claim.

Translations and automated assistance

The catalog has multiple language views of the same inventory. Comparison articles remain English unless a page explicitly says otherwise. Translation can lose detail; use the linked original terms to resolve ambiguity. Automated collection and text processing do not replace source checks.

Corrections

Report a correction in the public repository with the page URL, disputed statement and a primary source. Do not include account details, private receipts, personal contact information or other secrets in a public issue. Corrections can change conclusions; a review date alone is not proof that a claim is correct.

Search and AI use

Static page text, source links, structured data and a sitemap help systems interpret this site. They do not guarantee indexing, ranking, rich results or AI citations. The plain-text directory is an optional convenience, not a special search requirement.
