Clerk vs Auth0

At 50,000 Auth0 MAU, B2C Essentials costs $3,500/month; at 50,000 Clerk MRU, Pro costs $25/month: $3,500 / $25 = 140. Auth0 counts non-internal users who authenticated that month per tenant; Clerk counts users returning on a later day after signup per app, so these are not equivalent populations.

Verdict

Pick Clerk if most of your sign-ups never come back a second day, because Clerk bills only the ones who do — 50,000 MRU are included on a $25/month Pro plan. Pick Auth0 if your identity programme runs on enterprise SSO, many tenants and custom connections, and budget for the step: 501 B2C users cost $70 on Essentials, the same as 1,000.

1. Clerk and Auth0 count different people

Read the unit before the price. Each vendor defines it in its own words:

VendorUnitWho is counted
ClerkMonthly Retained User (MRU)visits at least one day after signup, per app
Auth0Monthly Active User (MAU)non-internal user authenticated that month, per tenant

Clerk counts the first day free. A signup that never returns is excluded from MRU, but counts toward MAU if non-internal and authenticated.

A consumer app with heavy sign-up churn will look cheap on Clerk, and part of that cheapness is definitional.

Vendor pricing pages, checked on 10 November 2026: Clerk Hobby includes 50,000 MRU per app for $0; Auth0 Free includes 25,000 MAU for $0.

2. Who should not pick Auth0's paid B2C plans

  • Clerk forbids a limitless free tier: Hobby stops at 50,000 MRU per app with no MFA or passkeys, and one month of grace before the app stops working.
  • Clerk forbids big free organisations: 100 MRO per app, 20 members each. An MRO has at least 2 members, one of them an MRU that month.
  • Auth0 forbids paying for exactly what you use. Between tiers you are billed at the next tier up.
  • Avoid Auth0 B2C Essentials and Professional if you need enterprise connections: both list them as "not available". Free includes 1; custom SSO tiers require Enterprise.

3. On Auth0, the 501st user costs $35

Auth0's B2C price is a staircase. You pay for the tier you land in:

At 500 MAU, Essentials is $35 / 500 = $0.07 per user. User 501 moves you to the 1,000 tier: $70 − $35 = $35 for one person. The $70 bill is double $35.

The usage punished is a spike that crosses a boundary. One launch week past a tier line bills the whole next tier. Professional has a harder cliff: $240 climbing to $3,200, then no self-serve price at all. Professional's printed prices end at 20,000 MAU for B2C and 10,000 for B2B.

Annual billing keeps the steps: yearly is 11 × monthly, so 50,000 MAU on Essentials is 11 × $3,500 = $38,500.

4. Who should not pick Clerk without budgeting add-ons

Clerk Pro is $25/month, or $20/month billed annually. It includes 50,000 MRU per app, then $0.02 per MRU with volume discounts. Every plan allows unlimited applications.

The add-ons move the bill:

Add-onPrice
SSO connection1 included, then $75/mo
Directory Sync1 included, then $75/mo from 1 January 2027
B2B Authentication: Enhanced add-on$100/mo; $1/MRO beyond 100 included per app on all plans; removes 20-member limit
Protect10,000 checks free, then $0.002/check; 1,000,000+ quote-only; brute-force prevention excluded

Ten enterprise customers, one SSO connection each, cost (10 − 1) × $75 = $675/month. From January 2027, Directory Sync for the same ten adds another (10 − 1) × $75 = $675.

Protect at 200,000 checks is (200,000 − 10,000) × $0.002 = $380.

Avoid Clerk's self-serve plans if you need a 99.99% SLA or a HIPAA BAA; both are Enterprise-only.

5. The 140x gap is correct arithmetic on two different quantities

Paid-plan estimates at equal numeric inputs, not an identical population. Auth0 counts MAU; Clerk counts MRU.

TIERS = [(500, 35), (1_000, 70), (2_500, 175), (5_000, 350), (7_500, 525),
         (10_000, 700), (20_000, 1_400), (30_000, 2_100), (40_000, 2_800), (50_000, 3_500)]

def auth0(mau):  # between tiers you pay the next tier up
    return next((p for cap, p in TIERS if mau <= cap), None)  # breaks: >50,000 unpriced

def clerk_pro(mru):  # ignores Clerk's volume discounts
    return 25 + max(0, mru - 50_000) * 0.02

for n in (501, 50_000, 100_000):
    print(n, auth0(n), clerk_pro(n))

At 50,000 non-internal MAU per tenant/month, Auth0 is $3,500; at 50,000 later-day MRU per app, Clerk is $25 + 0 × $0.02 = $25. $3,500 / $25 = 140. 140x prices 50,000 people who logged in against 50,000 who came back on a later day. Not the same crowd.

At 100,000 MRU, Clerk is 50,000 × $0.02 + $25 = $1,025/month. Auth0's B2C sheet ends at 50,000: "contact us".

6. Pick by who you are billed for, then check the next boundary

Choose Clerk Pro if you need MFA, passkeys or branding removal below 50,000 MRU per app; Hobby excludes them.

For 10 dashboard seats, 5,000 MRU and 200 qualifying MRO, Clerk Business costs $300 + $100 + (200 − 100) × $1 = $500/month. Auth0 B2B Essentials is $1,300 at 5,000 MAU, but Essentials and Professional allow only 10 organisations, not 200.

Choose Auth0 Professional if you need custom database connections or cross-app SSO; it includes 12 tenants.

What you gain, what you give up

Clerk

You gain
  • 50,000 MRU included on Pro, then $0.02 each — a user who never returns is never billed
  • Unlimited applications on every plan, including Hobby
  • Organisations and B2B authentication are in the same product, with 100 MRO included
  • Full data exports on every plan, so leaving is a data move rather than a negotiation
You give up
  • The bill hides in add-ons: SSO $75/mo each, Directory Sync $75/mo each from 1 January 2027
  • Hobby stops at 50,000 MRU after a one-month grace period
  • Protect checks meter at $0.002 after 10,000 free, so attack traffic is a billable event
  • Business is a $300/mo step mostly for SOC 2, seats and 30-day logs

Auth0

You gain
  • 25,000 MAU free, with 5 organisations, self-service SSO and SCIM included
  • B2B Essentials is $150/month for 500 MAU with unlimited organisations
  • Enterprise SSO connections included — 3 on Essentials, 5 on Professional — then $100/month each
  • Professional adds custom database connections, cross-app SSO and 12 tenants
You give up
  • Price is a step function: 501 B2C users bill at the 1,000 tier, $70 instead of $35
  • The B2C Professional sheet ends at 20,000 MAU; past that it is a quote or not available
  • Custom enterprise SSO on B2C requires Enterprise — not available on Essentials or Professional
  • The AI Agents add-on adds 50% to whichever tier it is applied to

Migration path

What switching actually takes

Clerk to Auth0 means exporting users, recreating connections and mapping organisations onto a plan that includes 5 on Free and 10 on paid tiers — well short of 200 without a higher tier. Auth0 to Clerk changes the unit: MAU becomes MRU, so the population you migrate is not the population you are billed for, and users who never return after day one vanish from the invoice.

Questions that change the decision

Does Auth0 have a free tier?
Yes: Auth0 Free is $0 up to 25,000 MAU, with 1 tenant, 5 organizations, 1 enterprise connection and 1-day log retention. Self-service SSO and SCIM are included on it.
Do I need Clerk Business to offer SSO?
No. Pro includes 1 SSO connection and charges $75/month for each additional connection; Business costs $300/month or $250/month billed annually and adds a SOC 2 report, 10 dashboard seats and 30-day application and admin logs.
How long does each vendor keep logs on the paid tiers?
Auth0 keeps 5 days on Essentials, 10 on Professional and 30 on Enterprise. Clerk Pro keeps 7 days of logs and Business keeps 30.
What does SMS login cost on Clerk?
SMS authentication is on Pro at $0.01 per SMS for the US and Canada, with international messages at market rate.
